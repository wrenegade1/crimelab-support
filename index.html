Privacy

Crime Lab stores your progress on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. There are no ads, no third-party trackers, and no account sign-up.

If you email support, your email address is used only to reply and is not kept beyond that conversation.

FAQ

Can I replay a case?
Cases are generated per playthrough — you can start a new one anytime, but a specific case won't come back the same way. That's the design.

Does it work on iPad?
Yes. iPhone and iPad, iOS 16 and newer. Portrait only for now.

How do I join the beta?
If you've been sent a TestFlight invite link, tap it on your iPhone or iPad and install Apple's free TestFlight app when prompted. If you'd like an invite, email me.
