Case File · Privacy Policy

Crime Lab

Effective October 9, 2026

Summary

Crime Lab does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, share, or trade any personal information. There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers in the app.

If that's all you need to know, you can stop reading.

What stays on your device

Your game progress — unlocked cases, saved detective-board state, badges and settings — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's standard on-device storage. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

If you use iCloud with your Apple ID, iOS may back up app data to your private iCloud account as part of standard device backups. That data is encrypted and controlled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

What we do not do

  • No account creation, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No ad networks, no ad SDKs, no ad personalization.
  • No analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no AppsFlyer, no third-party crash reporters).
  • No social-media SDKs, no cross-app tracking, no App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to track.
  • No in-app purchases that transmit personal data.
  • No contact-list, photos, location, microphone, camera, or health-data access.

If you email support

If you contact us at the email address listed on the support page, your email address and the content of your message are used only to respond to you. Messages are not shared with any third party and are retained only as long as needed to resolve your question.

Children

Crime Lab is suitable for a general audience. Because the app collects no data at all, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children under 13 (COPPA) or children under 16 in the EEA/UK (GDPR). No parental consent is required because there is nothing to consent to.

Your rights (CCPA / GDPR summary)

Because Crime Lab collects no personal information:

  • There is no personal data to request a copy of, correct, or delete.
  • There is no personal data sold or shared, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

If this changes in the future, this page will be updated and the effective date above will reflect the change.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync or in-app purchases — this policy will be revised before that version ships, with the effective date updated at the top of the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the same place as support questions: see the support page for the contact email.
