Letter Pegs · iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026. This page describes the current Letter Pegs beta.

Local game data

Letter Pegs saves your puzzle progress, guesses, scores, streaks, earned rewards, and settings on your device. The app does not operate a developer-hosted account or cloud-save service. Device backups are managed by your Apple settings. Deleting the app may delete local game data; we cannot restore progress from a developer server.

Optional Game Center

If you choose to connect to Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center identity and authentication. When you tap Sync Best Daily Score, the app sends your best eligible completed Daily score to Apple’s leaderboard. The score includes its final wheel reward. Your Game Center profile and leaderboard entry may be visible according to Apple’s Game Center settings.

Practice and developer testing scores are excluded. Game Center does not upload your puzzle-save file or provide cloud saves for Letter Pegs. You can play locally without connecting. See Apple’s Game Center privacy information.

Analytics, advertising, and permissions

The current app contains no developer analytics SDK, advertising SDK, third-party tracking SDK, or location collection code. It does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or location. Apple may process app diagnostics according to your device and service settings.

TestFlight beta testing

When you use TestFlight, Apple manages invitations and may share beta feedback, crash reports, screenshots you submit, and testing information with the developer. This is separate from a developer analytics service embedded in the app. Do not include sensitive information in feedback. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue. Avoid sending passwords, account credentials, or unrelated personal information. Contact tweeter_like5o@icloud.com for privacy questions or requests concerning support messages.

This website

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts or tracking cookies to the Letter Pegs pages. GitHub may process connection information when you visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link or sending email uses the policies of that service.

Changes

We will update this page when the app’s data handling changes. The date above identifies this version.
