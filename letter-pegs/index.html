Letter Pegs

Support for the five-letter word game on iPhone and iPad.

Need help?

Email tweeter_like5o@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. Screenshots can help; remove anything personal before sharing them.

How to play

Use the category clue to find a hidden five-letter word. Try any five letters, even a made-up word, then tap Try Word. Green pegs show exact matches; amber pegs show letters elsewhere in the answer; gray pegs show no remaining match. Each peg belongs to its letter, and duplicate letters are counted separately.

Daily puzzles have eight base attempts, points, streak bonuses, and a reward spin after a win or loss. Extra guesses or a revealed letter help the next new Daily. Practice is unlimited and unscored.

TestFlight access

Install Apple’s TestFlight app and accept your emailed invitation using the invited Apple Account. External testing starts after Apple approves the beta. There is currently no public invitation link; email support if you would like to join.

The beta follows normal Daily and spin limits. Unlimited testing is a developer-only mode and is not included in TestFlight builds.

Common questions

  • Where is my spin? Finish a Daily and tap Spin for a Reward on the results screen, or Claim Your Spin on Home.
  • Can I replay? Practice is unlimited. Normal Daily assigns one puzzle per local date and resumes unfinished puzzles.
  • Where is my progress? It is saved on this device. Game Center does not sync game progress across devices. Removing the app can remove its local progress.
  • Music or sound? Settings has separate Music and Sound FX switches and volume controls. Check your device’s silent switch as well.
  • Game Center unavailable? You can keep playing locally. Connect and retry score sync in Settings. The first beta’s leaderboard integration is still being verified.
